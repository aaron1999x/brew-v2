### Grind fields, no silently blocked input (`fix(recipes): clearer grind fields, no silently blocked input`)
- **Cause:** Aaron typed 7.5 into the grind *size* (µm) box by mistake and the "." never appeared, with no message. React Aria's `NumberField` filters keystrokes itself: with a whole-number format it simply refuses ".", and letters. That is the silent blocking.
