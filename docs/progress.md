- **`/dev/components` renders in every environment, production included.** Public by choice during development, so the design can be reviewed from any device without a staging URL. `/dev/logged-out` and `/dev/fullscreen` keep the production gate (`isDevToolsEnabled`, still unit-tested).
- **Kept out of search engines:** `robots: { index: false, follow: false }` in the page metadata, and a new `src/app/robots.ts` that allows everything except `/dev/`. `robots.txt` is excluded from the session-refresh proxy.
- **Notice at the top:** "Component gallery — placeholder design and sample data." (new small `Notice` component in the status-info colours).
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/dev-pages.test.tsx` (the gallery renders with `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_ENV` set to production, staging and local; the two other pages still throw `notFound` in production only), `tests/unit/robots.test.ts`, and e2e checks for the noindex meta, `robots.txt` and the notice. Also checked by hand against a production-mode build: `/dev/components` 200 with noindex, `/dev/logged-out` and `/dev/fullscreen` 404, `robots.txt` as expected.
- **Later:** a polished `/design-system` page comes after the design pass (OQ23). OQ23 is not in `docs/decision-log.md` yet (its open questions stop at OQ22); add it there when convenient. The gallery stays as the engineering reference until then.
